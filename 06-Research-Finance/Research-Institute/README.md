## Role

The Research Institute is a future coarse-grained research building for mechanism-first computer science:

```text
reality / external-domain mechanism
  -> ontology / mechanism abstraction
  -> AI formalization
  -> mathematical / algorithmic review
  -> multi-AI critique
  -> simulation
  -> experiment
  -> human mechanism review
  -> revision
```

The building exists in the city map so the PhD-oriented direction has an explicit home without becoming City Constitution or Boss Core.

## Planned program: Machine Intelligence

Initial research questions may include:

- delayed/post-learning consolidation;
- familiarity / fusion / maturity state;
- dynamic association structures;
- long-term adaptation without immediate permanent mutation;
- stability/plasticity trade-offs;
- AI-assisted mathematical formalization;
- falsifiable prediction and experiment loops.

Machine Intelligence is a **program inside Research Institute first**. It is not an independent city building yet.

## AI delegation model

Algorithmic AI may perform most formalization work:

- variable/parameter proposals;
- mathematical model construction;
- derivation;
- stability/sensitivity analysis;
- simulation;
- counterexample generation;
- code implementation;
- evidence packaging.

Human review retains the semantic/mechanism decision:

- do the variables correspond to the intended reality?
- do their interactions preserve the intended mechanism?
- what assumptions were lost?
- what result would falsify the model?

## Product-boundary rule

Do **not** pre-create separate Mathematics Bureau, Simulation Centre, Mechanism Lab, Adversarial Office, or similar products.

They are workflow roles until independent lifecycle/maintenance needs prove otherwise.

## Policy scope

A future Research Charter applies to Research only.

It must not automatically constrain Hns/Engineering, Medical, Entertainment, or other domains.

## Creation condition

A dedicated repository should be created only when real research work requires executable code/data/workflows that cannot be cleanly hosted in an existing research repository.

If the PhD route does not materialize, this placeholder may remain an optional personal research area or be archived without affecting city-core operation.
