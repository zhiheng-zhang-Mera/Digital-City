## Building role

该区域容纳市场、量化和可重复研究实验。它是领域建筑，不应把金融策略直接写进 Boss 的城市基础设施。

## Buildings and rooms

### [Quant-ultra](https://github.com/zhiheng-zhang-Mera/Quant-ultra)

- Quant research baseline.
- Strategy experimentation.
- Learning/gym-style evolution experiments.
- Cash-flow / asset-growth objective experiments.

### [ML-Quant-A-stock](https://github.com/zhiheng-zhang-Mera/ML-Quant-A-stock)

- A-share ML modeling.
- Data/model research.

### [Personal-trading-project-for-fun](https://github.com/zhiheng-zhang-Mera/Personal-trading-project-for-fun)

- Prototype strategies.
- Earlier trading experiments.

## Roads

- **Evidence Road** ↔ research evaluation.
- **Learning Road** ↔ bounded reusable learning infrastructure where non-domain-specific.
- **Capability Road** ↔ Boss for orchestration.

## Boundary

Market-specific assumptions, strategies, portfolios and model state stay inside finance/research buildings. Only reusable city-level learning/runtime primitives should move toward Boss.
