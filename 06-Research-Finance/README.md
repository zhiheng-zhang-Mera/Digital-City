## Roads

- **Evidence Road** ↔ research evaluation.
- **Learning Road** ↔ research learning experiments while they remain domain-scoped.
- **Capability Road** ↔ Boss for generic orchestration only.

## Boundary

Market-specific assumptions, strategies, portfolios and model state stay inside finance/research buildings. Research-specific mechanisms and charters remain here until explicit cross-domain evidence justifies promotion.

The Research Institute placeholder does not authorize implementation and does not create a dependency for Boss, Hns, Medical or any other building.
