# PCF-721 — Controlled systems study

[Canonical state](../PCF-721-controlled-systems-study.md) · [Research/release protocol](RESEARCH_AND_RELEASE.md) · [Shared steps](EXECUTION_CONTRACT.md). PARKED translation.

Propose a study-contract test, bilingual study protocol and PCF scenarios in the verified REX layout, not another experiment database.

After pilots freeze hypotheses, conditions, repetitions/stopping, analysis, hardware/cache/network controls and failure accounting. Compare compatible, capability-only, load-only and PCF policies under equal safety and consent floors. Use 724's real mixed workloads to measure contention, latency/throughput, SLOs, recovery, refusal/placement errors, owner intervention and overhead. Bind every trial to exact runtime/policy/executor/input identity.

Use actual REX fault APIs for authorized disconnection, worker crashes, stale/duplicate data and storage failure. Controller restart and worker failover are distinct from HA. Perform controlled ablation and independent physical-host reproduction. Retain failure, negative and no-benefit results, unknown data and unfavorable samples.

Export manifests, sanitized datasets, commands/scripts, raw references/digests, environment/versions, statistical outputs and limitations, including cache and clock handling.

Acceptance requires real runner/fault/replay/export integration and independent reproduction; missing runners/exports cannot become COMPLETE. Contract-only development may remain separate, but simulation cannot replace execution. Use at least two real software workloads. Glasses/health traces prove only their input scope, not physical-device or clinical performance.

This study grants no main-merge authority. Reserved 790 accepts the complete core/user path. Two-worker evidence cannot establish datacenter-scale or universal-device claims.
