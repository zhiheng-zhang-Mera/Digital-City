## Objective

Expose Research Fabric to Owner without overloading normal UI. Follow [RESEARCH_CONTROL_SURFACE.md](RESEARCH_CONTROL_SURFACE.md).

## Minimum entry

One clear secondary `Research / Experiments` entry, organized into Experiments, Runs, Metrics, Replay/Ablation, Export, Advanced Fault Injection, Technical Details.

## Mandatory validation

Normal Home/Ask/Devices remain free of research-control overload; Research works without console/API-only use; high-impact faults resist accidental activation; raw IDs collapsed by default; errors/exclusions/incomplete metrics visible. Web is full control surface; Android at least observes run/status/critical attention, with complete authoring parity recordable in future backlog.

## Review

Formal Reviewer uses normal-user paths to find hidden entries, fake buttons, excessive collapsing, insufficient information, and visual overload.

## Completion gate

Direct control, awareness, dangerous-action isolation, and collapsed technical details all satisfy global Capability Exposure Gate.
