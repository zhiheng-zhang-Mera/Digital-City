## Building role

这一建筑类型横跨全城，类似**数据保护机构 + 法律/审计设施**。它不负责所有业务数据处理，但定义和验证隐私、证据、研究复现和数据使用边界。

## Buildings and rooms

## Roads

- **Privacy Road** → Residential, Medical, Device Infrastructure.
- **Evidence Road** → Government/qualification and Research.
- **Publication Road** → cleaned external research artifacts.

## Boundary

Privacy governance is cross-city, but domain semantics remain with the originating building. This building should define constraints and evidence, not absorb health, resident, finance or device logic.
