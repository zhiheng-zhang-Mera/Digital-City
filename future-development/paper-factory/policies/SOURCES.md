## Interpretation limits / 解释限制

Access failures included HTTP access/cache errors. No third-party mirror was promoted to official authority. The unavailable targets receive meaningful local content/evidence design, but no unverified numeric limits, review model, capsule platform or fees are asserted as official requirements.

SPE's conflicting source-upload and keyword passages remain unresolved. Preprints.org's multi-server preference is different from a prohibition. JOSS's task-specific AI restriction is different from a blanket ban on AI software development. IEEE Access's recommended length is different from a hard page ceiling. / 这些差异是适配器必须保留的语义，不能压成一个真假位。

This set is not exhaustive: publisher-wide authorship/ethics, conference open-science details, article-specific rules, the live portal, special issues, AI subpolicies and publication costs still require target-specific final verification. / 有官方来源也不表示整个目标已核验完毕。
