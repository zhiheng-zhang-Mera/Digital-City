# City Infrastructure — 城市基础设施

STATUS = STRUCTURE_READY

This area contains capabilities that exist for the whole city rather than one business/domain district.

## Slots

- [Core OS](./Core-OS/) — city substrate/runtime slot.
- [Node Fabric](./Node-Fabric/) — shared device/node identity, membership and transport/coordination contracts.
- [Roads](./Roads/) — cross-city contract catalogue.
- [Control Centre](./Control-Centre/) — human-facing city observation/navigation/control surface.

## Boundary

City Infrastructure must stay thin. Domain applications, domain data and domain-specific quality rules remain in their owning districts/buildings.
